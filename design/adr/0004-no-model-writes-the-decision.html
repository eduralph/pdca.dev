Design documentation

0004. No model writes §9; accept is refused while a NEEDS-HUMAN item is open (C6)

Context

A model grades its own work leniently and will mark work done without end-to-end evidence. If the accept decision can be written by a model, every other check in the cycle is advisory. The maintainer's sign-off is the one step the harness exists to protect.

Decision

  • One writer. signoff.record (template/src/pdca_harness/signoff.py:255) is the only code that writes §9, and the driver's decision step is its only caller. Leaves propose a decision token; the human decides; the driver records it.
  • C6 accept-guard. accept is refused while any - [ ] row is open under the last ## 6. NEEDS-HUMAN heading of SUMMARY.md (signoff.open_needs_human). A SUMMARY with no §6 section cannot be evaluated and is refused (signoff.unrecordable).
  • Auto-iterate is narrow. The automatic loop may only ever write iterate-do, only while an implementation-level finding exists, and never past max_auto_iters; it defers findings that need a person instead of ticking them.
  • Merging is human. No leaf may mark a PR ready, merge it or approve it; a gh shim and a PreToolUse hook (builder_guard) enforce this.

Present from the first commit (3ca179a, 2026-06-04); the guard was extended by the park disposition (#42) and the unverifiable class (#46) in June 2026.

Consequences

Acceptance is code, not a prose verdict, and no model output can release a publish on its own. The human carries the sign-off load, so the quality of §6 decides whether the guard is read or rubber-stamped (risk R2 in architecture §11).

Known gap. The interactive sign-off session runs with Edit/Write, and nothing yet stops it editing §9 directly; state.state would read that text as decided. The guard there is the prompt and a watching human. #710 closes this with a hook.
