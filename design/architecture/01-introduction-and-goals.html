1. Introduction and goals

Living document. What pdca-harness is and why, as of main @ d6057eb (2026-10-15). Open questions are marked [OPEN] inline.

1.1 What this is

pdca-harness is a Copier template that renders a Plan → Do → Check → Act quality cycle into a software project. Each AI-driven contribution becomes one bundle: a written brief (Plan), a patch plus a test that fails before and passes after (Do), deterministic gates, a reviewer from a different model vendor and a human sign-off (Check), and a cross-cycle process review (Act). A deterministic Python driver, pdca, moves every bundle through the cycle; models are invoked only at fixed points ("leaves") and never decide the control flow. The walkthrough in docs/00-introduction.md explains the problem in full.

1.2 The headline promise

No model can accept its own work. The accept decision is recorded by code, from a human's decision, and is refused while any NEEDS-HUMAN item is open (ADR-0004). Everything else in the harness exists to make that decision well-informed and cheap.

1.3 Quality goals

When goals conflict, the lower number wins.

# Goal What it means here
1 Integrity of the accept Only a human decision accepts a bundle; evidence, not prose, blocks it (ADR-0004, ADR-0006).
2 Independent review The reviewer's errors are independent of the builder's (ADR-0005).
3 Resumability Any crash or interrupt is resumed by running the same command again (ADR-0003).
4 Maintainer attention Every item put in front of the human needs a human; ritual items are a defect (risk R2).
5 Adaptability One template serves projects with different trackers, branches, gates and toolchains (ADR-0007).
6 Cost awareness Work is ranked by what it saves, measured in tokens and time. [OPEN] nothing is recorded yet (#711).

1.4 Stakeholders and target users

  • Maintainers who take AI-generated contributions into a real codebase and sign them off.
  • Instance operators who render the template, concretize it and run pdca flow — today the wyrd, pdca-pdca (the harness building itself), autobook and nl-shell instances.
  • Harness contributors, who work through the pdca-pdca instance.

1.5 Scope boundaries

In scope: the cycle, its artifacts, the driver, the leaf prompts, gates protocol, batching (waves, lanes, integration lines), publishing draft PRs, and the Act review.

Not goals: merging PRs (always human), hosting or scheduling runs, being a model vendor's agent framework, or replacing a project's CI.
