4. Solution strategy

Living document. The shape of the solution and why. Open questions are marked [OPEN] inline.

4.1 The shape in one paragraph

A deterministic driver advances file-based bundles through four beats. Models fill artifacts at six leaves inside those beats — planner, builder, reviewer, sign-off assistant, publisher, Act — plus optional advisory, sizer and splitter leaves. The driver owns every transition, every gate and the accept guard; leaves only produce files. Batches of bundles run in waves (ordered by declared Depends on / Conflicts with edges) across parallel lanes, each in its own git worktree, and accepted bundles are published as draft PRs, optionally stacked on a shared integration line.

4.3 Build strategy

The target is the six-layer architecture of proposal 0002: records, a pure machine, effects, roles, policies and orchestration, with typed outcomes. It is reached by a strangler migration (ADR-0016); §9 holds the order.
