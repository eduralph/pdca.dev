Recorded here because accepted proposals are not edited:

  • Cost is measured in tokens, not dollars. Wherever 0001 and 0002 say "cost", "dollars", total_cost_usd or max_budget_usd, the accepted reading is token counts (input, output, cache read, cache write) and a max_tokens budget. See #711.
  • Instances are not pinned. 0002's "instances stay pinned at v0.59 until the step-1 renderer passes golden tests" is not adopted; instances keep following main (ADR-0016).
